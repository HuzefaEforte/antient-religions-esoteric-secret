## Summary

A comparative essay tracing the **esoteric (inner) layer** of the major religions — the "shell and
kernel" model — from Islam (*zahir*/*batin*, [[tawil]], [[wahdat-al-wujud|haqiqa]]) outward to
**Hinduism, Judaism (Kabbalah), Christianity, Buddhism, Taoism**, the Hermetica, and the Greek
Mysteries, with brief nods to Jainism and Sikhi. It argues the esoteric core of these traditions
converges on **[[non-duality]]** — *"the seeker is the sought"* — while **honestly preserving the
places they disagree**. It closes with a "full decoding" of scripture (Part 8) and a set of
first-person pointers to awareness, "the eye that cannot see itself" (Part 9).

Where [[S0001]] wrapped the same core in a tech/"matrix" metaphor and a wealth/manifestation
program, **S0002 drops all of that** and stays with the metaphysics — and adds the scholarly
caveats S0001 omitted.

## Key claims

- **Shell & kernel.** Every tradition has an outer form (for all) and an inner teaching (taught
  quietly to the prepared). Method vs. destination: **[[tawil]]** (carrying back to origin;
  *tanzil* is revelation coming down, *ta'wil* going back up) → **[[wahdat-al-wujud|haqiqa]]** (the
  Reality). `[tradition]` [[S0002]]
- **Four shared claims** of the esoteric core (Buddhism dissenting): (1) reality is not-two;
  (2) what you are is the aware ground, not the ego — *the seeker is the sought*; (3) it is
  **structurally unsayable** (hence [[via-negativa|negation]] everywhere); (4) the path is
  **[[remembrance|remembering, not acquiring]]**. `[esoteric]` [[S0002]]
- **New traditions vs. S0001:** **[[kabbalah|Judaism/Kabbalah]]** (PaRDeS; Ein Sof; sefirot;
  Luria's tzimtzum/tikkun); **[[christian-mysticism|Christianity]]** (*mysterion*; [[gnosticism]];
  [[pseudo-dionysius|via negativa]]; theosis; **[[meister-eckhart]]**); **[[buddhism]]**
  ([[two-truths]]/shunyata; **[[nagarjuna]]**); **[[taoism]]** (**[[laozi]]**). `[tradition]` [[S0002]]
- **Honest dissent (the corrective S0001 lacked):**
  - Buddhism's **anatta** (no-self) negates the Hindu **[[atman-brahman|Atman]]** — "genuinely
    rival final answers." `[scholarly]`/`[tradition]` [[S0002]]
  - **Wahdat al-shuhud** (unity of *witnessing*, Ahmad Sirhindi) as the orthodox correction to Ibn
    ʿArabi's [[wahdat-al-wujud]]: the servant stays a servant. `[tradition]` [[S0002]]
  - **Perennialists** (Huxley, Guénon, Schuon, Nasr) vs. **contextualists** (Steven Katz): is there
    one summit, or does each tradition build its own peak? `[scholarly]` [[S0002]]
- **Emanation vs. intent**, richer than S0001: **al-Ghazali vs. Avicenna** (*kun fa-yakun* / "Be!"
  vs. necessary overflow); Aquinas — God creates *freely* yet *without deliberation* (divine
  simplicity); the "third thing," **[[lila|play / love]]**. `[scholarly]` [[S0002]]
- **The two decoding keys** (Part 8): whatever is "long ago / end of time" is happening **NOW**;
  every character/place in scripture is inside **YOU**. Applied to God, Creation, Adam/Eden/Fall/
  Iblis, prophets, rituals, heaven/hell → the "biography of the one consciousness," hide-and-seek.
  See [[esoteric-decoding]]. `[esoteric]` [[S0002]]
- **The eye that cannot see itself** (Part 9): tongue can't taste itself; grandma's glasses; the
  cinema screen; the fish in the ocean; "closer than the jugular vein." Pointers to
  **[[the-observer|awareness as subject, never object]]**. `[esoteric]` [[S0002]]
- **Historical origin** (Part 6): burial ~100k ya, cave art ~40k ya, Göbekli Tepe ~9,600 BCE, the
  **[[axial-age|Axial Age]]** (800–200 BCE) as the "inward turn." Time is *part of* creation
  (Augustine; "north of the North Pole"). `[scholarly]` [[S0002]]

## Contradictions / tensions

- **Top-level disagreement is real** (S0002's own Footnote 1): *"You are it"* (Advaita) vs.
  *"you unite but stay distinct"* (most Sufis, Christian mystics, Sirhindi) vs. *"there is no you"*
  (Buddhism). S0002 refuses to flatten these — the wiki follows suit. See [[convergence-and-its-critics]].
- **Convergence is contested** (Footnote 2): perennialists vs. contextualists (Katz). S0002 does
  *not* claim the convergence proves a single truth — a marked improvement over S0001's "science
  proves it" rhetoric.
- **Still `llm-generated`.** Its many attributions (Quran 3:7; Mark 4:11 / Luke 17:21; PaRDeS;
