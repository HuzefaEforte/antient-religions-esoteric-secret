# Hermeticism

**Hermeticism** is the Western esoteric tradition attributed to **[[hermes-trismegistus]]**,
centered on the idea that the universe is mental and that the microcosm mirrors the macrocosm. In
[[S0001]] it supplies the **[[seven-hermetic-principles]]** and the "As above, so below" axiom.

## Core ideas (per S0001)

- **Mentalism** — "The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental." Basis for [[non-duality]] framed as
  "consciousness creates matter."
- **Correspondence** — "As above, so below" — the human as a miniature universe; tied to the
  Oracle of Delphi's *"Know Thyself."*
- The full seven-fold law (mentalism, correspondence, vibration, polarity, rhythm, cause-effect,
  gender) is documented at [[seven-hermetic-principles]].

## Cautions
- [scholarly] Two things the source conflates: (1) the **ancient Hermetica** (Hellenistic Egyptian
  texts, c. 1st–3rd c. CE) and (2) **[[kybalion|The Kybalion]]** (1908) — a **modern New Thought**
  book that *presents* the seven principles. Much of what [[S0001]] calls "ancient Hermetic
  secret" is actually early-20th-century. This matters for the wider caution in
  [[perennialism-and-modern-overlay]].
