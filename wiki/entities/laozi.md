# Laozi

**Laozi** ("the Old Master"), the traditional author of the ***Tao Te Ching***, is the founding
figure of [[taoism|Taoism]] in [[S0002]].

## In S0002

[tradition] Credited with the opening declaration *"The Tao that can be spoken is not the eternal
Tao"* — the sharpest statement of [[via-negativa]] — and, via the *Tao Te Ching*, with the vision
of the Tao as the nameless, unmanifest source (paralleled by [[S0001]] with [[plotinus|Plotinus's]]
"The One"). See [[taoism]].

## Caution
- [scholarly] Laozi's **historicity is debated**; the *Tao Te Ching* may be a composite text. Noted
  so the legendary author isn't treated as a documented biography.
