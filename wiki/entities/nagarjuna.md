# Nagarjuna

**Nagarjuna** (c. 150–250 CE), founder of the **Madhyamaka** ("Middle Way") school of Mahayana
Buddhism, is cited in [[S0002]] as the formulator of the **[[two-truths]]** doctrine.

## In S0002

[tradition] Nagarjuna's two truths — **conventional** (*samvriti*) and **ultimate** (*paramartha* =
**shunyata**, emptiness) — structure [[buddhism]]'s version of shell/kernel: nothing exists
*independently*, though things are real enough conventionally. This underwrites Buddhism's principled
dissent (*anatta* vs. [[atman-brahman|Atman]]) developed on [[convergence-and-its-critics]].
