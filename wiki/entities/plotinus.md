# Plotinus

**Plotinus** (c. 204–270 CE) was the founder of **Neoplatonism** and, in [[S0001]], "the
grandfather of the cascade system" — the ultimate source of the [[emanation]] doctrine later taken
up by Islamic philosophers.

## Contribution

[scholarly] In the ***Enneads***, Plotinus describes reality overflowing from **The One** (the
absolute, indivisible source) into the **Divine Mind / Intellect (Nous)**, then the **World Soul**,
and finally **Matter** — each level a diminished reflection of the one above. This is the blueprint
that **[[al-farabi]]** and **[[ibn-sina]]** reworked into the Islamic **[[emanation|Ten
Intellects]]**. His *Enneads* is the first entry in the source's reading list for the "cascade."

## Role in the synthesis
- Cited in [[S0001]]'s **"independent convergence"** argument as the Greek witness (c. 200 CE) to
  the same "One → many" architecture found in the [[upanishads]] and later Sufism — see
  [[perennialism-and-modern-overlay]] for the caution on that argument.
