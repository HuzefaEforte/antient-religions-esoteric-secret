# Rumi

**Jalal al-Din Rumi** (1207–1273 CE), the Persian Sufi poet (*Mevlana*) whose followers became
the whirling **Mevlevi** order, appears in [[S0001]] chiefly as the **posthumous defender of
[[al-hallaj]]** and a voice for [[fana]] and [[the-observer]].

## In S0001

[tradition] Because Rumi was born ~300 years after al-Hallaj's execution, he could not defend him
in life but did so through poetry. The source quotes (attributed to *Fihi Ma Fihi*):

> "People think saying 'I am the Truth' is a claim of great pride. But it is a declaration of
> absolute humility… He has drowned in the ocean, and nothing remains of him but the water."

- The **whirling dervish** is used as an image of [[the-observer]]: the skirt spins (the changing
  world), the central axis stays still (the witness anchored in the Now).
- Also quoted (widely attributed to Rumi): *"You are not a drop in the ocean; you are the entire
  ocean in a drop."* — see [[atman-brahman]], [[non-duality]].

## Cautions
- Popular Rumi quotations circulate loosely; exact wording/attribution (the *Fihi Ma Fihi* passage
  and the "ocean in a drop" line) should be verified against a `scholarly`/`primary` translation.
