# Hermes Trismegistus

**Hermes Trismegistus** ("Thrice-Great Hermes") is the legendary author-figure of the Hermetic
writings, a syncretic fusion of the Greek **Hermes** and the Egyptian **Thoth**. In [[S0001]] he
is the named source of the **Emerald Tablet** and its maxim "As above, so below."

## In S0001

[esoteric] The source cites the **Emerald Tablet** (*Tabula Smaragdina*) for the first Hermetic
law, "**The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental**," and treats "As above, so below" as the key to
[[seven-hermetic-principles|Correspondence]]. See [[hermeticism]] and [[non-duality]].

## Syncretism (a domain hub)

[scholarly] Hermes Trismegistus sits at a classic syncretic identification:
- **Thoth** (Egyptian god of writing/wisdom/the moon) ↔ **Hermes** (Greek) ↔ **Hermes
  Trismegistus** (the Hellenistic-Egyptian sage).

*This is exactly the kind of cross-tradition identity the wiki prizes (see `CLAUDE.md` §3.2). When
a dedicated **Thoth** entity is created, link it here and record the identification on both pages.*

## Cautions
- [scholarly] The Hermetic texts are **Hellenistic (c. 1st–3rd c. CE)**, not remote antiquity; and
  the maxim "The All is Mind" as phrased comes via the modern **[[kybalion|Kybalion]]** (1908), not
  a verbatim ancient tablet. The source blurs these. See [[kybalion]].
