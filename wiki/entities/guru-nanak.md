# Guru Nanak

**Guru Nanak** (1469–1539), founder of **Sikhi**, appears in [[S0002]] as a deliberate
**counter-move** to esoteric secrecy.

## In S0002

[tradition] Where other traditions guarded the inner teaching, Nanak's ***Ik Onkar*** ("One
Reality/God") states the non-dual truth **openly**, and hands the method — **Naam simran**
(remembrance of the Name) — to everyone, not an initiated elite. See [[non-duality]],
[[remembrance]].

This makes Sikhi a useful contrast case for [[esoteric-decoding]] and [[convergence-and-its-critics]]:
the "open secret" made fully open. [[S0002]] notes the teaching survives being stated plainly
because it "was never information."
