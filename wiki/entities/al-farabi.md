# Al-Farabi

**Al-Farabi** (c. 872–950 CE), "the Second Teacher" (after Aristotle), was a founding figure of
Islamic Neoplatonism. In [[S0001]] he is a principal architect of the **[[emanation|Ten
Intellects]]** cosmology.

## Contribution

[scholarly] Al-Farabi mapped the descent from the **First Existent** (*mawjud al-awwal*) — perfect,
uncaused, immaterial — through a series of **secondary intellects**, each contemplating itself and
the First Existent and thereby causing the next intellect and its celestial sphere, down to the
**Active Intellect** that governs the sublunary world. This is exactly the structure diagrammed in
image 7 of the raw source (a modern UML rendering of "Al-Farabi Cosmology"). See [[emanation]].

## Connections
- Developed further by **[[ibn-sina]]** (Avicenna); both ultimately draw on **[[plotinus]]**.
- The [[tayyibi-ismailism|Tayyibi Ismaili]] **[[drama-in-heaven]]** is a mythic reworking of this
  same intellect-hierarchy.
