# Mansur al-Hallaj

**Mansur al-Hallaj** (c. 858–922 CE) was a Persian Sufi mystic, executed in Baghdad, famous for
the ecstatic utterance **"Ana al-Haqq"** (أنا الحق — "I am the Truth / the Real"). In [[S0001]]
he is the emblem of [[non-duality]] spoken aloud, and of the price of speaking it.

## "Ana al-Haqq"

[tradition] Because *al-Haqq* ("the Truth/Reality") is one of the 99 Names of God, literalist
scholars (*Ulema*) read the phrase as a man claiming to be God — blasphemy. The esoteric reading
in [[S0001]] is the opposite: spoken from complete **[[fana|ego-death]]**, it meant *"Mansur does
not exist at all; only the Divine exists"* — an utterance of the Divine *through* an emptied
vessel, aligned with [[wahdat-al-wujud]].

- Explanatory images used: **iron glowing in fire** ("I am the fire!") and the **hollow flute**
  (the music is the musician's breath, not the reed) — see [[fana]].
- Image 5 in the raw source is an **Isha Foundation (Sadhguru)** illustration of al-Hallaj
  *dancing* to his execution.

## Execution (922 CE)

[tradition] Arrested and imprisoned for ~9 years, then tortured (hands and feet cut off) and
executed. Accounts have him **smiling and dancing** to the gallows, praying for his executioners —
because he identified with [[the-observer]] (the screen), not the body (the movie).

- [tradition] The source relates the **prophecy of the two robes**: his teacher **Junayd of
  Baghdad**, required to co-sign the death warrant, removed his Sufi robe and donned a jurist's,
  ruling *"by the external law, Mansur is guilty; as for the inner truth, only God knows."*
  (Detail attributed to Sufi/Sadhguru retellings; `tradition`/`llm-generated` — corroborate.)

## Legacy

- [tradition] **[[rumi]]** (b. 1207, ~300 years later) could not defend him in life but did so in
  verse — *Fihi Ma Fihi*: "the one who says 'I am the Truth' has emptied himself completely… nothing
  remains of him but the water." See [[rumi]].

## Contradictions / cautions
- Dates for his birth vary (c. 858 CE commonly); the source sometimes rounds his floruit to the
  "10th century." The prophecy-of-robes and dancing-execution details come from devotional
  retellings — treat as `tradition`, not established biography, until corroborated.

## Corroboration & context (S0002)

[[S0002]] corroborates the ego-death reading of *Ana al-Haqq* ("a wave shouting 'I am the ocean!'")
and places al-Hallaj in a **lineage of mystics punished for speaking the inner truth**:
- **Suhrawardi** (the Illuminationist) executed **1191**; **[[meister-eckhart]]** condemned by papal
  bull **1329** — "the Christian echo of Hallaj's fate."
- This is *why* Sufi poets **encrypted** everything — "wine" = divine love, "the Beloved" = God;
  **Shabistari** titled his masterwork *The Rose Garden of Secret*. See [[esoteric-decoding]] ("why
  it was written in code").
