# Isaac Luria

**Isaac Luria** ("the Ari," 1534–1572), the great Safed kabbalist, is credited in [[S0002]] with
the dramatic cosmology of **tzimtzum** — central to [[kabbalah|Lurianic Kabbalah]].

## In S0002

[tradition] Luria's myth of creation:
- **Tzimtzum** — the Infinite (**Ein Sof**) **contracted itself** to make room for a world.
- **Shevirah** — the vessels meant to hold the divine light **shattered**.
- **Sparks & Tikkun** — divine **sparks** scattered into everything, awaiting **tikkun** (repair),
  making every good deed potentially cosmic.

## Connections
- The "contraction to make room for an other" answers *why create* through love/relationship
  ("no king without a people") — see [[lila]].
- The **scattered sparks awaiting repair** parallel the [[gnosticism|Gnostic]] spark-in-matter and
  the fallen souls of the [[drama-in-heaven]] — a strong cross-tradition [[emanation]] echo.
