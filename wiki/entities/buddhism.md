# Buddhism

**Buddhism** enters the wiki via [[S0002]] as the tradition that makes the same structural move as
the others (an inner truth behind conventional appearance) **while dissenting from their
conclusion** — no God, no permanent Self.

## Core ideas (per S0002)

[tradition]
- **[[two-truths|The two truths]]** (**[[nagarjuna]]**): conventional (*samvriti*) vs. ultimate
  (*paramartha* = **shunyata**, emptiness — nothing exists *independently*).
- **Buddha-nature** — already present in all beings, "like the sun behind clouds"; uncovered, not
  built. **Zen**'s wordless "flower sermon" as transmission (see [[via-negativa]]).
- **Anatta** (no-self) — the load-bearing dissent: it **negates** the Hindu [[atman-brahman|Atman]].

## The dissent (why it matters)

[scholarly] Buddhism is [[S0002]]'s **"honest dissenter."** It refuses "one God / one Reality"
language at almost every turn; *anatta* vs. *Atman* are *"genuinely rival final answers."* It also
denies a creator or cosmic purpose ([[emanation]], [[lila]]) and **refused the origin question**
(the poisoned-arrow parable; see [[axial-age]]). Yet it agrees the everyday "me" is the illusion to
see through — so it lands in the same *practical* place: wake up, now. Central to
[[convergence-and-its-critics]].
