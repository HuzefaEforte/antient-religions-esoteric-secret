# Taoism

**Taoism** enters via [[S0002]] as the Chinese instance of the ineffable Absolute and inner
practice.

## Core ideas (per S0002)

[tradition]
- **The ineffable Tao:** *"The Tao that can be spoken is not the eternal Tao"* — the opening line
  disqualifies language ("like describing sweetness to someone who's never tasted sugar"). The
  strongest statement of [[via-negativa]]. Attributed to **[[laozi]]** (the *Tao Te Ching*).
- **Neidan** (inner alchemy) — later Taoism encoded contemplation in a **cipher of furnaces and
  elixirs** (a Chinese parallel to Western [[hermeticism|alchemical]] encryption; cf. the
  [[ouroboros]]).
- **Ziran** ("self-so") — creation as things arising spontaneously, *"like flowers blooming without
  a business plan."* [[S0002]]'s Taoist answer to *why creation*: **no reason at all — and that is
  the answer.** See [[lila]], [[emanation]].
