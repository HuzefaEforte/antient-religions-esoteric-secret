# Pseudo-Dionysius

**Pseudo-Dionysius the Areopagite** (fl. c. 500 CE), an anonymous Christian Neoplatonist, is cited
in [[S0002]] as the fountainhead of Christian **apophatic** theology.

## In S0002

[tradition] His *via negativa* — **"knowing by unknowing," the "divine darkness"** — is the
Christian form of approaching God by negation, part of the orthodox mystical core alongside
**theosis** (see [[christian-mysticism]], [[via-negativa]]). Historically he transmits Neoplatonic
([[plotinus|Plotinian]]) apophaticism into Christian thought.
