# Tayyibi Ismailism

**Tayyibi Ismailism** is a branch of Shia **Ismaili** Islam (its later community includes the
**Bohras**) whose esoteric cosmology [[S0001]] credits as the specific source of the
**[[drama-in-heaven]]**.

## In S0001

[esoteric]/[tradition] The Tayyibi cosmology is presented as formalized by 12th-century Yemeni
mystics — **Ibrahim al-Hamidi** and **Idris ʿImad al-Din** — building on the philosopher **Hamid
al-Din al-Kirmani**, and later studied by the French scholar **Henry Corbin** (*Cyclical Time and
Ismaili Gnosis*). Distinctive features:

- A **gnostic myth of a cosmic fall**: the **Third Intellect** hesitates, is displaced to the
  **Tenth**, and physical matter (and human souls) forms from its fallen "companions." See
  [[drama-in-heaven]].
- Time itself is a **consequence of the fall** (per Corbin) — history as the drama of the souls'
  return. Ties to [[emanation]] and [[non-duality]].
