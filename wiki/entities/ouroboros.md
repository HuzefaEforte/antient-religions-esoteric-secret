# Ouroboros

The **Ouroboros** — a serpent devouring its own tail — is an ancient symbol of cyclical unity,
used in [[S0001]] as the emblem of the "ultimate secret" that highest and lowest, spirit and
matter, are one continuous whole.

## In S0001

[esoteric] The head (the highest pole, pure spirit) and the tail (the lowest pole, dense matter)
look like opposites but belong to **one organism** — the perfect image of the
**[[seven-hermetic-principles|Principle of Polarity]]** and of [[non-duality]]. The cosmic story
(the [[drama-in-heaven|fall]] into matter and the [[fana|return]] to the Source) is "the snake
swallowing its tail" — the One stepping down into the low pole in order to wake and climb back up.
Image 9 in the raw source is a classic Ouroboros engraving.

## Notes
- [scholarly] Genuinely ancient (Egyptian funerary texts; later Greek alchemy and Gnostic use) —

  Boehme/alchemical-hand images (see [[S0001]]).
