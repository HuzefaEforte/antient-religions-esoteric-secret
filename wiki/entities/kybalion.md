# The Kybalion

**The Kybalion** (1908), published pseudonymously by "Three Initiates" (widely attributed to
**William Walker Atkinson**), is the modern New Thought book that codifies the
**[[seven-hermetic-principles]]**. [[S0001]] leans on it heavily and recommends it as the "starting
point" for the Hermetic material.

## In S0001

[esoteric] Cited for:
- The seven principles (mentalism, correspondence, vibration, **polarity**, rhythm, cause-effect,
  gender) — see [[seven-hermetic-principles]].
- **"Mental Transmutation"** — changing one's mental "vibration" to reshape circumstance; the
  bridge to the source's later "bend the matrix" material.

## Important caution
- [scholarly] Despite its ancient-Egyptian styling and the **[[hermes-trismegistus|Hermes
  Trismegistus]]** framing, the *Kybalion* is a **1908 New Thought** work — **not** an ancient
  Hermetic text. Treating its principles as "ancient secrets" (as [[S0001]] does) is an
  anachronism. See [[hermeticism]] and [[perennialism-and-modern-overlay]].
