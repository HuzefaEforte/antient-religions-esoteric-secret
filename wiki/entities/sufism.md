# Sufism

**Sufism** (*Tasawwuf*; the philosophical/Shia-inflected strand is *Irfan*) is the mystical,
esoteric dimension of Islam — the primary Islamic tradition drawn on throughout [[S0001]].

## Core ideas (as they appear in S0001)

- **The inner path:** the ascent **Shariat → Tariqat → Haqiqat → Marifat**, climbed via
  **[[tawil]]** (esoteric interpretation of the *batin*, the inner meaning). `[tradition]`
- **[[wahdat-al-wujud]]** — the Unity of Being (**[[ibn-arabi]]**): only the Divine truly exists.
- **[[fana]]** — annihilation of the ego, the door to that realization; and **Baqa** (subsistence).
- **Practices:** *Dhikr* (remembrance), especially silent heart-*dhikr* (*Dhikr-e-Qalb*); breath
  awareness (*Hosh dar Dam*, Naqshbandi order); these appear as methods of entering
  **[[the-observer|the timeless Now]]**.
- **Exemplars:** **[[al-hallaj]]** ("Ana al-Haqq"), **[[rumi]]** (Mevlevi whirling), the esoteric
  reading of **[[imam-hussain]]**.

## Relations
- The [[tayyibi-ismailism|Ismaili]] cosmology of the **[[drama-in-heaven]]** overlaps Sufi/Shia
  esotericism; the philosophical backbone comes from **[[emanation|Neoplatonic emanation]]**.
- Paralleled with **[[advaita-vedanta]]** across [[S0001]] — see [[islam-hindu-esoteric-parallels]].

## Corroboration & enrichment (S0002)

[[S0002]] adds the *zahir*/*batin* (outer/inner) framing and the **tanzil → ta'wil** movement (see
[[tawil]]); the poets' **encryption** ("wine" = divine love, "the Beloved" = God; Shabistari's *Rose
Garden of Secret*); more martyrs (**Suhrawardi**, 1191; see [[al-hallaj]]); the **[[al-ghazali|al-Ghazali]]**
vs. Avicenna debate on creation ([[emanation]]); and the orthodox **wahdat al-shuhud** (Sirhindi)
corrective to [[wahdat-al-wujud]] — see [[convergence-and-its-critics]].
