# The Upanishads

The **Upanishads** are the philosophical/mystical texts at the end of the Vedas — the scriptural
core of **[[advaita-vedanta]]** and, in [[S0001]], the Hindu vessel of *Gupta Vidya* (secret
knowledge).

## In S0001

[tradition]
- The word **Upanishad** ≈ "sitting down closely near a master" — teachings not shared with the
  general public. They form the **Jnana Kanda** (knowledge portion), as opposed to the **Karma
  Kanda** (ritual portion). See [[tawil]].
- The **Mandukya Upanishad** supplies the **[[four-states-of-consciousness]]** (waking / dreaming /
  deep-sleep / **Turiya**) and the identity **[[atman-brahman|Atman = Brahman]]**.
- Home of the *Mahavakyas* the source quotes — *Aham Brahmasmi*, *Tat Tvam Asi*. The **Nasadiya
  Sukta** (Hymn of Creation) it quotes on cosmic origins is actually from the **Rig Veda**, not the
  Upanishads — noted so the two aren't conflated.
