# Christian mysticism

The inner/contemplative stream of Christianity, introduced by [[S0002]] as another instance of the
shell/kernel pattern — the *mysterion* given to the inner circle.

## Core ideas (per S0002)

[primary]/[tradition]
- **Mysterion** — Jesus tells his inner circle they are given the *mysterion* of the kingdom while
  outsiders get parables (Mark 4:11); "the kingdom of God is **within** you" (Luke 17:21).
- **[[gnosticism|The Gnostics]]** — (Nag Hammadi texts, unearthed 1945): know yourself all the way
  down and you find God.
- **The orthodox mystical core** — **[[pseudo-dionysius]]**'s *via negativa* ("divine darkness,"
  knowing by unknowing; see [[via-negativa]]); the Eastern Church's **theosis** — *"God became
  human so that humans could become god-like."* A Christian cognate of [[fana]]→baqa and *moksha*.
- **[[meister-eckhart]]** — the ground of the soul and the ground of God are *one single ground*;
  condemned by papal bull (1329) — the Christian echo of [[al-hallaj]]'s fate.
- **The cross, decoded** — a vertical line (eternity) crossing a horizontal (time), meeting in the
  human being; *"Not I, but Christ lives in me"* read as [[fana]]/baqa in Christian code (see
  [[esoteric-decoding]]).

## Tension
- [tradition] Most Christian mystics hold **union-with-distinction**, not strict identity (the soul
  unites with God but remains creature) — closer to *wahdat al-shuhud* than to Advaita. See
  [[convergence-and-its-critics]].
