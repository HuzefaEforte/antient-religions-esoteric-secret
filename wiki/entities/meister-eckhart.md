# Meister Eckhart

**Meister Eckhart** (c. 1260–1328), German Dominican mystic, appears in [[S0002]] as Christianity's
most radical non-dual voice — and its "Hallaj," condemned for it.

## In S0002

[tradition]
- Preached that **the ground of the soul and the ground of God are *one single ground*** — a
  Christian near-approach to [[non-duality]] / [[wahdat-al-wujud]]. See [[christian-mysticism]].
- **Condemned by papal bull in 1329** (shortly after his death) — [[S0002]] calls it "the Christian
  echo of [[al-hallaj]]'s fate" (see [[esoteric-decoding]], "teachers were killed for it").
- His image of creation as **ebullitio** ("boiling over") and God creating the world **now** feeds
  [[lila]] and [[emanation]] (creation as continuous expression, not a past event).

## Caution
- [tradition] Whether Eckhart taught strict identity or union-with-distinction is itself debated;
  he sits at the contested boundary discussed on [[convergence-and-its-critics]].
