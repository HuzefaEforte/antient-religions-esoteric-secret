# Gnosticism

**Gnosticism** is a family of early esoteric movements (largely Christian-adjacent, Hellenistic)
centered on **gnosis** — saving self-knowledge. It appears in **both** sources, a rare cross-source
node.

## In the sources

- [[S0002]]: the **Gnostics** (texts buried at Nag Hammadi, dug up in **1945**) taught: *know
  yourself all the way down, and you find God* — a Christian instance of "the seeker is the sought"
  ([[non-duality]], [[christian-mysticism]]). Their texts were "buried in a jar to survive"
  persecution (see [[esoteric-decoding]], "why it was hidden").
- [[S0001]]: recommends **Hans Jonas's *The Gnostic Religion*** — the Gnostic view of the physical
  world as an artificial construct (a "matrix") ruled by **Archons**, from which the inner "spark of
  light" can awaken and remember its origin. [[S0001]] maps the Archons onto its Active Intellect /
  [[drama-in-heaven]] cosmology (`llm-generated` framing).

## Connections
- The "divine spark trapped in matter, awaiting awakening" motif parallels the [[drama-in-heaven]]
  (fallen souls / companions) and Kabbalah's scattered **sparks** ([[kabbalah]], [[isaac-luria]]).
- Self-knowledge → God ties to *"Whoever knows himself knows his Lord"* ([[sufism]], [[the-observer]]).
