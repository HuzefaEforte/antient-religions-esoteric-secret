# Convergence and its critics

The hub page for the wiki's central question: **do the esoteric cores of the traditions actually
converge on one truth — and if they seem to, what does that mean?** Built from [[S0002]], which
supplies the honest **dissent** that [[S0001]] flattened. This page holds both the claimed
agreement *and* the disagreements, as first-class content.

## The claimed convergence — four shared claims

[esoteric] Put the summit-statements side by side — *"You are That"* / *"I am the Real"* / *"the
kingdom is within you"* / *"your ground and God's ground are one"* / *"Buddha-nature is already in
you"* — and [[S0002]] distills **four** recurring claims (Buddhism dissenting on #2):

1. **Reality is not-two.** Felt separateness is the root error — [[maya]], *avidya*, *ghaflah*,
   sin-as-separation, ignorance, sleep. → [[non-duality]]
2. **What you are is the aware ground, not the ego** — and that ground is not other than the Real.
   *The seeker is the sought.* → [[the-observer]], [[atman-brahman]]
3. **It cannot be captured in words** — the knower can't become its own object; hence negation
   everywhere. → [[via-negativa]]
4. **The path is remembering, not acquiring.** → [[remembrance]]

## The honest dissent (why it does NOT simply collapse into one)

[scholarly]/[tradition] The traditions **disagree at the very top** — genuinely rival final
answers, not details ([[S0002]] Footnote 1):

| Position | Who | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Strict identity** | [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita]] | *You **are** it* (Atman = Brahman) |
| **Union-with-distinction** | most [[sufism|Sufis]], [[christian-mysticism|Christian mystics]], Sirhindi | you unite with it but **stay distinct** (servant remains servant) |
| **No-self** | [[buddhism|Buddhism]] | there is **no "you"** at all (*anatta*) — see [[two-truths]] |

Two specific dissents this wiki now records inline as well as here:
- **Anatta vs. Atman** — Buddhism's no-self *negates* the Hindu Self. → [[two-truths]], [[atman-brahman]]
- **Wahdat al-shuhud vs. wahdat al-wujud** — Ahmad Sirhindi's "unity of *witnessing*" as the
  orthodox correction to Ibn ʿArabi's "unity of *being*." → [[wahdat-al-wujud]]

## What the convergence *means* — the scholarly split

[scholarly] [[S0002]] Footnote 2 names the real academic debate — which this wiki adopts as its own
open question:

- **Perennialists** (Aldous **Huxley**, René **Guénon**, Frithjof **Schuon**, Seyyed Hossein
  **Nasr**): *one summit, many trails* — a single perennial truth behind the forms.
- **Contextualists** (**Steven Katz** and others): *there is no unmediated experience* — each
  tradition **constructs its own peak**; apparent convergence is partly an artifact of
  interpretation, shared influence (e.g. Neoplatonism shaping several), and selection.

## Bearing on the wiki's thesis

The [[overview|working thesis]] (non-duality as common core) is **the perennialist reading**. This
page keeps the contextualist objection permanently in view so the thesis is held as a *hypothesis*,
not a result — reinforced by the reliability caveat that both current sources are `llm-generated`
(see [[perennialism-and-modern-overlay]]). Corroborating or challenging this is the top priority for
future `scholarly`/`primary` ingests.
