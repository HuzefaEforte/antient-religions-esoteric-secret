# The Axial Age

The **Axial Age** (Karl Jaspers's *Achsenzeit*) names the period **c. 800–200 BCE** when, per
[[S0002]], the "inward turn" happened across several civilizations that never met — the historical
"birthday" of the esoteric knowledge.

## The claim

[scholarly] Between 800–200 BCE, humanity shifted from *"how do we please the gods?"* to *"what am
I, really?"* — among the **Upanishadic sages**, the **Buddha**, **[[laozi|Laozi]]**, the **Hebrew
prophets**, and the **Greek philosophers**, independently. [[S0002]] sets this in a longer arc of
the religious impulse:
- ~100,000 ya — deliberate burial with ochre and grave-goods ("you don't pack supplies unless you
  believe there's a journey").
- ~40,000 ya — deep-cave spirit paintings (ceremony, not decoration).
- ~9,600 BCE — **Göbekli Tepe**: monumental temples built *before* farming ("temples before
  villages; the impulse came first").
- Writing (~5,000 ya) is immediately religious (Egypt's Pyramid Texts; the oral Rig Veda).

## Tension

- [tradition] The traditions **reject the idea of a birthday**: Hinduism's *Sanatana Dharma* ("the
  eternal way"); Islam's claim that every prophet taught one truth; "Before Abraham was, I am." The
  *teachings* appear in history; the *truth* is held to be timeless. See [[esoteric-decoding]]
  (Key 1: it is happening NOW) and [[emanation]] (creation as continuous, "now").
- [scholarly] The Axial-Age thesis is itself **debated** in current scholarship (how synchronous or
  causally linked these movements really were). Flagged as an open question on [[S0002]].
