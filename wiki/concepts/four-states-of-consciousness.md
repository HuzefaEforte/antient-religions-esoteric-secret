# The Four States of Consciousness

A framework from the **Mandukya Upanishad** (see [[upanishads]]) mapping human experience into
four states, used in [[S0001]] to show that your true self is the unchanging witness behind all
experience — the Hindu route to [[non-duality]].

## The four states (avasthas)

[tradition] as presented in [[S0001]] (image 3):

1. **Jagrat** — the **waking** state; the mind engages the material world through body and senses;
   here you feel wholly separate.
2. **Svapna** — the **dreaming** state; the mind builds an entire world from its own subconscious.
   On waking you realize *you were* the mountain, the river, and the other people — all mind.
3. **Sushupti** — **deep, dreamless sleep**; no ego, no objects, no desire — pure latent potential.
4. **Turiya** — "the fourth"; not a state among the others but the **pure, unchanging background
   consciousness** that witnesses the other three.

[tradition] Turiya is identified with **[[atman-brahman|Atman]]** (true self), which — being
boundless — is identical to **[[atman-brahman|Brahman]]** (the Absolute): *Aham Brahmasmi*. All
four are wrapped in **[[maya]]**, the illusion that makes the one appear as many. [[S0001]]
