# The Drama in Heaven

The **Drama in Heaven** (*al-Drama al-Kawniyya*) is a cosmological myth [[S0001]] attributes
specifically to **[[tayyibi-ismailism|Tayyibi Ismaili]] cosmology** (formalized by 12th-century
Yemeni mystics such as **Ibrahim al-Hamidi** and **Idris ʿImad al-Din**, building on **Hamid
al-Din al-Kirmani**, and studied in the West by **Henry Corbin**). It explains why the smooth
[[emanation|emanationist cascade]] broke, and where physical matter — and human souls — came from.

## The myth

[esoteric]/[tradition] as told in [[S0001]]:

1. **The station.** After the First and Second Intellects, the **Third Intellect** emerges as a
   magnificent archangelic being, meant to acknowledge the two Intellects above it.
2. **The mistake (*al-Waqfa*, "the hesitation").** Instead of instantly recognizing the hierarchy,
   the Third Intellect **hesitated** — dazzled by its own light, it entertained the thought of
   self-sufficiency and tried to bypass the ranks above it to reach the Source directly. Not a
   moral sin but an **error of cosmic theology** — the first appearance of pride/separation
   (*Tark al-Awla*, "failing to choose the highest").
3. **The fall.** While it hesitated, the overflow kept flowing; **seven other Intellects emanated
   ahead of it**, displacing it to the **Tenth** position — becoming the **Active Intellect**
   ([[emanation]]).
4. **The birth of matter.** A group of subordinate "companions" believed the Third's illusion and
   fell with it. Their collective "darkness" **condensed and hardened into physical matter** — the
   stars, the earth, and human bodies.
5. **Repentance & rescue.** Realizing its fall, the Tenth Intellect repents and is appointed
   **Governor / Giver of Forms** of the physical world — an "incubator" built to wake and
   rehabilitate the fallen companions.

## The human meaning (per S0001)

- [esoteric] **Humanity *is* the fallen companions.** Your sense of separation and struggle
  re-enacts the primordial hesitation; every time you become [[the-observer]], you help correct
  the mistake. Matter is "frozen fallen light"; the body is clay, but the **soul** is the
  brightest companion-remnant and the **mind** a terminal of the Active Intellect (see the
  soul/mind/body tiering in [[S0001]]).
- [esoteric] **The end.** When the last soul awakens, matter "thaws," the Tenth Intellect rises
  back to the Third, and all Intellects re-collapse into the First and the Source — the *Ma'ad*
  / *Apocatastasis* / "Great Resurrection" (*Qiyama al-Qiyamat*). See [[non-duality]].
