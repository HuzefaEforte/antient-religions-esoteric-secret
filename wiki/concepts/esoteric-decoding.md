# Esoteric decoding — the two keys and hide-and-seek

The interpretive scheme of [[S0002]] Part 8: a way of reading scripture's *batin* (inner meaning)
using **two keys**, and the "biography of the one consciousness" it assembles. An applied form of
**[[tawil]]** that maps every sacred image onto present, first-person experience.

## The two keys

[esoteric] [[S0002]]:
> **Key 1** — whatever is described as "long ago" or "at the end of time" is actually happening **NOW**.
> **Key 2** — every character, place, and object in scripture is actually inside **YOU**.

## Sample decodings (per S0002, all `[esoteric]`)

- **"God"** = the one awareness in which everything appears — "the eye cannot see itself" (Moses's
  name **"I AM"**; the Quranic pair **Az-Zahir / Al-Batin**, Outer/Inner). See [[the-observer]].
- **"Creation"** = *this instant* — Genesis light *before* the sun = awareness before objects; the
  world as *speech in progress* (*kun*, Logos, Om); the nightly dream as a private *tzimtzum* (see
  [[kabbalah]]).
- **Adam / Eden / Fall / Iblis** = you / unity-consciousness / the mind dividing into duality / the
  **ego** (Iblis, "the first creature to insist on *I*"). The "expulsion" recurs around age two as
  open awareness contracts into "ME"; the *covenant of Alast* = awareness knew its source before
  individuation — hence truth feels like **[[remembrance|remembering]]**.
- **Rituals** train one muscle — shrinking the "me": prostration as **[[fana]]** in miniature; the
  Kaaba as the heart circled through seven veils; fasting, zakat (the law of flow), baptism,
  eucharist, the cross (eternity crossing time in a human being).
- **Heaven/hell/judgment** = **distances**, not places: heaven = nearness to the Real; hell = the
  burning of *feeling* separate (the ego is the fuel); Judgment = all veils falling — hence *"die
  before you die."* Egypt's heart weighed against a **feather** = lightness = no ego to weigh.

## The assembled story: hide-and-seek

[esoteric] Unity → the One veils itself (creation and "the fall" are the *same event* from two
sides) → forgetting → **longing** (every desire is homesickness for the One in a mask — "wrong
address," so no acquisition satisfies) → the call → the path ([[remembrance|dhikr]]) → the meeting
(the drop realizes it never left the ocean) → "to Him we return" — *a return in which nobody
travels anywhere.* **"The universe is the One playing hide-and-seek with itself — and you are the
hiding place."** See [[non-duality]], [[hide-and-seek|this page's aliases]].
