# Remembrance — the path is remembering, not acquiring

[[S0002]]'s **fourth shared claim**: across traditions the spiritual path is *undoing a
forgetting*, not gaining something new. "Nothing new is gained; a forgetting is undone." *The
seeker turns out to be the sought.*

## The theme across traditions

[tradition]/[esoteric]
- **Islam:** *dhikr* — remembrance (of God); the heart's repetition (see [[sufism]], [[the-observer]]).
- **Hinduism:** *pratyabhijna* — "recognition" (Kashmir Shaivism): God is an actor so absorbed in
  playing "you" that he forgot he is the actor; waking is simply remembering (see [[maya]], [[advaita-vedanta]]).
- **Greek/Platonic:** *anamnesis* — all learning is recollection (Plato); truth, when touched,
  *"never feels like learning, but like remembering."*
- **Judaism:** *teshuvah* — "return."
- **Sikhi:** *Naam simran* — remembrance handed openly to everyone by **[[guru-nanak]]**.

## Connections

- Grounds the "covenant of *Alast*" reading in [[esoteric-decoding]] (souls answered "Yes" before
  time; birth is the forgetting) and the hide-and-seek biography of consciousness.
- Complements **[[via-negativa]]** (the *method* is negation) with the *direction* of the path
  (return/recollection). Ties to [[fana]]/baqa and "die before you die."
