# Dream interpretation (Ta'bir)

Ancient literature treated dreams as meaningful transmissions rather than neural noise. [[S0001]]
gives a Sufi-inflected framework (*Ta'bir*, associated with **Ibn Sirin**) for classifying and
decoding them, and applies it to a specific dream the human reported.

## The framework

[tradition] **Tripartite classification** (the "source check") of a dream's origin:

| Category | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Ruyah** (true dream) | Divine / Intellect | clear, prophetic; leaves peace/certainty |
| **Nafsani** (soul dream) | Subconscious | daily-life residue, desires, anxieties |
| **Shaytani** (distraction) | Lower ego | nightmares, confusion, fear |

[tradition] **Decoding** uses a *"hermeneutics of similarity"* — reading the *quality* an image
embodies, not a fixed dictionary. Family members = traits of one's own psyche; animals =
"vibrational signatures" (lion = sovereign intellect/ego; dog = loyalty/attachment). Method of
**incubation**: purge the day, plant the question as intent, and on waking attend to the *feeling*
more than the imagery. `[tradition]`/`[esoteric]` [[S0001]]

## Worked example (the curator's dream)

The human described a **snake blocking a water-pipe path**, then a **crocodile seizing the snake
by the head** and carrying it into the jungle. [[S0001]] reads it as:
- **Water/pipe** = life-force / sustenance (*Rizq*) and its channel; **blockage** = stagnation.
- **Snake** = a hidden lower-ego pattern (*Nafs*) choking the flow.
- **Crocodile** = deep unconscious/"shadow" power acting as protector (Egyptian/Sufi symbol of
  sovereign, primal force) — grabbing "by the head" = neutralizing the blockage at its root.
- **Meaning:** a subconscious clearing of a stuck pattern; expect a release/opening.
`[esoteric]`/`[speculative]` — a symbolic reading, not a prediction. [[S0001]]
