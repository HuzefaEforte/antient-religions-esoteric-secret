# Non-Duality

**Non-duality** is the claim that the apparent separation between the observer and the
observed — self and world, creator and creation — is not ultimately real: at the deepest level,
reality is a single, continuous whole. In [[S0001]] it is named **"the greatest secret"** that
ancient esoteric traditions were said to guard, and it is the through-line of the entire source.

## The core claim

[esoteric]/[tradition] The boundary you feel between "me" and "everything else" is a mental
construct; when the ego dissolves, only the one reality remains. [[S0001]] frames this as a
change of *perspective*, not a hidden fact — a wave realizing it is the ocean.

Recurring analogies used in [[S0001]] (all `[esoteric]` teaching images, not arguments):
- **Wave and ocean** — the wave fears drying up until it realizes it *is* the ocean.
- **Cinema screen** — you are the untouched screen, not the burning/drowning movie (see [[the-observer]]).
- **Gold** — crown, map, and ring look different but are one substance.
- **Water as steam / liquid / ice** — Source, Intellects, and matter as one "fabric" at different densities.
- **Ouroboros** — highest and lowest poles belong to one organism ([[ouroboros]]).

## How it appears across traditions

- **[[advaita-vedanta]] (Hindu):** *Aham Brahmasmi* ("I am the Absolute"); *Tat Tvam Asi*
  ("That Thou Art"). The individual self ([[atman-brahman|Atman]]) is identical to
  [[atman-brahman|Brahman]]; the world of separate objects is [[maya]]. `[tradition]` [[S0001]]
- **[[sufism]] (Islamic):** [[wahdat-al-wujud]] ("Unity of Being," Ibn ʿArabi) — nothing truly
  exists but the Divine; creation is a mirror of God's attributes. `[tradition]` [[S0001]]
- **[[hermeticism]] / Neoplatonism:** "The All is Mind" (Kybalion); Plotinus's One from which all
  emanates. `[esoteric]`/`[scholarly]` [[S0001]]

The *identification* of these as one doctrine is [[S0001]]'s interpretive synthesis — see
[[islam-hindu-esoteric-parallels]] and the caution in [[perennialism-and-modern-overlay]].

## Tensions / variants

- [speculative] [[S0001]] argues quantum field theory (matter as ripples in universal fields;
  atoms as "empty space") and the **Default Mode Network** going quiet in meditation *prove*
  non-duality. These are **analogies and correlations, not proofs**; treat as rhetorical, not
  established. `[llm-generated]`
- Non-duality (Advaita) is not the only Hindu view — qualified non-dualism (Vishishtadvaita) and
  dualism (Dvaita) reject strict identity of self and God. [[S0001]] does not mention these; a
  gap to fill with a `scholarly` source.
- Within Sufism, *Wahdat al-Wujud* was itself contested (vs. *Wahdat al-Shuhud*, "unity of
  witnessing"). Not noted by the source. See [[wahdat-al-wujud]].

## Corroboration & dissent (S0002)

[[S0002]] **corroborates** non-duality as the shared esoteric core and reframes it as **four shared
claims** (reality is not-two; you are the aware ground, not the ego; it is unsayable → [[via-negativa]];
the path is [[remembrance]]). But it adds the honesty S0001 lacked:
- **Buddhism dissents** on claim 2: *anatta* (no-self) rejects a Self/God to identify with (see
  [[two-truths]], [[buddhism]]).
- The traditions **disagree at the summit** — strict identity (Advaita) vs. union-with-distinction
  (most Sufis/Christian mystics) vs. no-self (Buddhism).
- **Scholars are split** on what the convergence *means* — perennialists vs. contextualists.

All of this is gathered on **[[convergence-and-its-critics]]**. Note: S0001 and S0002 are **both
`llm-generated`**, so their agreement is not independent scholarly corroboration.
