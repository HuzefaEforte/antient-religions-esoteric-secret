# Ta'wil — esoteric interpretation

**Ta'wil** (Arabic: تأويل) is the practice of reading scripture and reality for their **inner,
symbolic meaning** rather than their literal surface. In [[S0001]] it is the master "decoding
tool" of Islamic esotericism, paired with its Hindu analogue, *Gupta Vidya* (hidden knowledge).

## Definition

[tradition] Ta'wil literally means **"leading something back to its origin."** It is contrasted
with **Tafsir**, which explains a verse's literal, historical, grammatical meaning. Ta'wil assumes
the words, stories, and laws of holy texts are **codes** for deeper psychological and cosmic
truths. [[S0001]]

- **Example given:** the parting of the sea is read not as past history but as an inner process —
  *Moses* = the intellect/soul, *Pharaoh* = the arrogant lower ego (*Nafs*), the *sea* = chaotic
  material desire. `[esoteric]` [[S0001]]
- **The four-station ascent** (image 1): **Shariat** (outer law) → **Tariqat** (the path, under a
  master) → **[[wahdat-al-wujud|Haqiqat]]** (direct experience of Reality) → **Marifat** (ultimate
  gnosis). Ta'wil is the means of climbing it. `[tradition]` [[S0001]]

## Across traditions

- **[[sufism]] / [[tayyibi-ismailism|Ismaili]] (Islam):** Ta'wil; the *batin* (inner) vs. *zahir*
  (outer). Central to Shia and especially Ismaili thought. `[tradition]` [[S0001]]
- **[[advaita-vedanta]] (Hinduism):** *Gupta Vidya* — the "secret knowledge" of the
  **[[upanishads]]** (the word *Upanishad* ≈ "sitting closely near a master"). Decoding tools:
  *Nirukta* / *Paroksha* (indirect/hidden meaning). Karma Kanda (ritual) vs. Jnana Kanda
  (knowledge). `[tradition]` [[S0001]]

The one-to-one mapping (Shariat↔Karma Kanda, Ta'wil↔Nirukta, Haqiqat↔Brahman) is [[S0001]]'s
comparison — filed in [[islam-hindu-esoteric-parallels]].

## Tensions

- [scholarly] The *concept* of an inner/outer layer is well attested; but the source's specific

## Corroboration & enrichment (S0002)

[[S0002]] sharpens the mechanics: revelation coming *down* is **tanzil**; interpretation carrying it
*back up* is **ta'wil** — "like walking upriver to find the spring." It grounds *zahir*/*batin* in
**Quran 3:7** (clear vs. ambiguous verses; "none knows their ta'wil except God…"), notes the
polemical label **batiniyya**, and adds two parallels:
- **[[kabbalah|Kabbalah]]'s PaRDeS** — Peshat/Remez/Derash/**Sod** (secret) — the Jewish four-level
  version of outer→inner reading.
- Its **applied form** — the "two keys" that decode scripture onto present experience: see
  **[[esoteric-decoding]]**.
