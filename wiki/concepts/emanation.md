# Emanation (Sudur / Fayd) and the Ten Intellects

**Emanation** is the doctrine that the many arise from the One not by deliberate manufacture but
by **overflow** — as light necessarily radiates from a source. In [[S0001]] it is the backbone
cosmology, given in the classical Islamic-philosophical form of the **Ten Intellects**.

## The doctrine

[scholarly] Originating with **[[plotinus]]** (*The One → Intellect (Nous) → Soul → Matter*) and
developed by **[[al-farabi]]** and **[[ibn-sina]]** (Avicenna), emanation solves a logical
problem: *how can a perfectly simple, unified God give rise to a multiple, complex world without
compromising divine unity (Tawhid)?*

The answer: **from the One, only one thing can proceed directly.** God's overflow produces a
single **First Intellect** (*Aql al-Awwal*). [[S0001]] gives its threefold self-contemplation:

1. Contemplating its **Source** → generates the **Second Intellect**.
2. Contemplating its own **necessity** → generates the **Cosmic/Universal Soul**.
3. Contemplating its own **limitation** → generates the first **celestial sphere** (matter).

This "copy-paste" repeats down a cascade of intellects and planetary spheres until the **Tenth
Intellect**, the **Active Intellect** (*Aql al-Faʿal*, the *Giver of Forms / Wahib al-Suwar*),
which stamps forms onto the matter of the sublunary world — our earth. `[scholarly]` for the
structure; `[llm-generated]` for the "server/GPU/source-code" gloss [[S0001]].

- **Terms:** *Sudur* and *Fayd* both name the "overflow." [[S0001]] stresses (via [[ibn-sina]])
  that emanation is **automatic and eternal**, like heat from fire — though [[sufism|Sufi]] and
  theological voices insisted it was an **intentional act of love** (see [[wahdat-al-wujud]] and
  the "Hidden Treasure" hadith in [[non-duality]]). The source reconciles them: for the timeless
  Divine, *intent* and *process* are the same act.

## Esoteric identifications (from S0001)

- [esoteric] The **First Intellect** is also called the *Supreme Pen (Al-Qalam al-Aʿla)* and the
  *Reality of Muhammad (Haqiqat al-Muhammadiyyah)* — a cosmic blueprint, not the historical man.
- [esoteric] The human mind is a "terminal" of the **Active Intellect**; insight ("Aha!" moments)
  is described as tuning into it. This maps the cosmology onto psychology — [[S0001]]'s move, not
  a traditional claim.

## Tensions / variants

- The smooth ten-step cascade is **complicated by the [[drama-in-heaven]]**, the Tayyibi Ismaili
  variant in which the Third Intellect *falls* to become the Tenth. [[S0001]] presents both the
  smooth version and the fallen version in different turns without fully reconciling them.
