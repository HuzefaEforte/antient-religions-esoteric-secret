# Wahdat al-Wujud — the Unity of Being

**Wahdat al-Wujud** ("the Unity / Oneness of Being") is the Sufi doctrine, most associated with
**[[ibn-arabi]]**, that **nothing truly exists except the Divine**; the universe is a mirror
reflecting God's names and attributes. In [[S0001]] it is the Islamic face of [[non-duality]] and
the endpoint (*Haqiqat*) of the esoteric ascent.

## The doctrine

[tradition] There is only one Real Existence (*al-Wujud*, ≈ *[[al-hallaj|al-Haqq]]*). Created
things have no independent existence; they are *loci of manifestation* (*mazahir*) through which
the hidden Divine becomes known. [[S0001]] ties this to the **"Hidden Treasure" hadith qudsi**:
*"I was a Hidden Treasure, and I loved to be known; so I created the creation that I might be
known"* — the Divine places a mirror (the cosmos) before itself to experience its own attributes.

- **Haqiqat** ("the Truth/Reality") is the station where the seeker directly *realizes* this,
  beyond blind faith — the third rung of the [[tawil|Shariat→Tariqat→Haqiqat→Marifat]] ladder.
- Related Sufi ideas in [[S0001]]: **[[fana]]** (annihilation of the ego) as the experiential
  door to this realization; **[[al-hallaj|"Ana al-Haqq"]]** as its most radical public utterance.

## Relation to other traditions

- Presented as equivalent to **[[advaita-vedanta]]**'s Atman=Brahman and to Hermetic "All is
  Mind" — see [[non-duality]] and [[islam-hindu-esoteric-parallels]]. The equation is the
  source's synthesis. `[esoteric]`/`[llm-generated]` [[S0001]]

## Tensions

- [scholarly] Wahdat al-Wujud was **contested within Islam** — e.g. the rival "Unity of
  Witnessing" (*Wahdat al-Shuhud*), and orthodox charges that it collapses the Creator/creation
  distinction. [[S0001]] presents it as *the* Sufi view without noting the dispute — a gap to fill
  with a `scholarly` source.

## Corroboration & dissent (S0002)

[[S0002]] corroborates Wahdat al-Wujud (creation as God's continuous self-showing, *tajalli* — "one
ocean appearing as millions of waves") and the maxim *"Whoever knows himself knows his Lord."* It
also supplies the **orthodox correction** this page flagged as missing:
- **Wahdat al-shuhud** ("unity of *witnessing*," Ahmad **Sirhindi**): in the end the servant remains
  a servant and the Lord remains the Lord — union *with distinction*, not identity of being. This is
  now recorded as a first-class dissent on **[[convergence-and-its-critics]]**.
