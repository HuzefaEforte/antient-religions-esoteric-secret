# The Seven Hermetic Principles

The seven principles set out in **[[kybalion|The Kybalion]]** (1908), presented in [[S0001]] as
"the laws of physics for the mind." Note: though styled as ancient Hermetic wisdom, the *Kybalion*
is an **early-20th-century** New Thought work (see [[hermeticism]], [[kybalion]]).

## The seven principles

[esoteric] as summarized in [[S0001]]:

1. **Mentalism** — "THE ALL is Mind; the Universe is Mental." Mind is primary; matter is condensed
   thought. Ties to [[non-duality]] ("consciousness creates matter").
2. **Correspondence** — "As above, so below; as below, so above." The macrocosm mirrors the
   microcosm; the human body is a miniature universe (linked by the source to the Oracle of
   Delphi's *"Know Thyself"*). Enables reading the cosmos by looking inward.
3. **Vibration** — "Nothing rests; everything vibrates." Matter, thought, and emotion differ only
   in *frequency*; "mental transmutation" = raising one's vibration.
4. **Polarity** — "Everything is dual; opposites are identical in nature, differing in degree."
   Hot/cold, light/dark, fear/courage are one spectrum. Illustrated by the thermometer (cold =
   low heat) and darkness (= absence of light) — so one "raises the dial" rather than fighting the
   negative pole. The source ties polarity to the **[[ouroboros]]** and the "ultimate secret" that
   separation is illusory (see [[non-duality]]).
5. **Rhythm** — "Everything flows, out and in; all things rise and fall." The pendulum swings;
   the master "neutralizes" by staying anchored as [[the-observer]].
6. **Cause and Effect** — "Every cause has its effect; chance is a name for law not recognized."
   Become the cause, not the effect.
7. **Gender** — "Everything has its Masculine and Feminine principles." Will (projector) + the
   subconscious (womb/matrix) generate a new reality.
