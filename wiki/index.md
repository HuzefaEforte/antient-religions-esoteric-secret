## Entities

### Figures
- [[al-hallaj]] — Sufi martyr (d. 922) executed for "Ana al-Haqq"; emblem of ego-death.
- [[ibn-arabi]] — Andalusian Sufi (1165–1240); Wahdat al-Wujud (Unity of Being).
- [[rumi]] — Sufi poet (1207–1273); posthumous defender of al-Hallaj.
- [[al-farabi]] — Islamic Neoplatonist (c. 872–950); architect of the Ten Intellects.
- [[ibn-sina]] — Avicenna (c. 980–1037); emanation as necessary overflow.
- [[al-ghazali]] — Theologian (c. 1058–1111); creation as free act (*kun fa-yakun*) vs. the philosophers.
- [[plotinus]] — Founder of Neoplatonism (c. 204–270); "The One" and the original emanation.
- [[imam-hussain]] — Martyr of Karbala (680); read esoterically as pure intellect vs. ego.
- [[hermes-trismegistus]] — Legendary Hermetic sage (Thoth↔Hermes); Emerald Tablet.
- [[meister-eckhart]] — Christian mystic (c. 1260–1328); soul's ground = God's ground; condemned 1329.
- [[pseudo-dionysius]] — Christian apophatic theologian (c. 500); the *via negativa*.
- [[nagarjuna]] — Buddhist philosopher (c. 150–250); the two-truths / emptiness.
- [[isaac-luria]] — Kabbalist (1534–1572); tzimtzum, shattered vessels, tikkun.
- [[laozi]] — Legendary founder of Taoism; the *Tao Te Ching*.
- [[guru-nanak]] — Founder of Sikhi (1469–1539); the non-dual truth stated *openly*.

- [[sufism]] — Islamic mysticism; Ta'wil, Wahdat al-Wujud, Fana, Dhikr.
- [[advaita-vedanta]] — Hindu non-dualism; Atman=Brahman, Maya, the four states.
- [[tayyibi-ismailism]] — Ismaili branch credited with the "Drama in Heaven."
- [[hermeticism]] — Western esoteric tradition of Mentalism & Correspondence.
- [[kabbalah]] — Jewish mysticism; PaRDeS, Ein Sof, sefirot, tzimtzum/tikkun.
- [[christian-mysticism]] — Mysterion, theosis, via negativa, Eckhart.
- [[gnosticism]] — Gnosis / self-knowledge; Nag Hammadi; the spark in matter. *(both sources)*
- [[buddhism]] — Two truths, emptiness, Buddha-nature — the honest dissenter (*anatta*).
- [[taoism]] — The ineffable Tao; inner alchemy (*neidan*); *ziran*.

### Texts

- [[kybalion]] — 1908 New Thought book codifying the seven Hermetic principles (not actually ancient).

## Concepts
- [[non-duality]] — The claimed "greatest secret": observer and observed are one. *(hub)*

- [[the-observer]] — Witnessing awareness; "the eye that cannot see itself"; the Eternal Now.
- [[two-truths]] — Nagarjuna's conventional/ultimate; emptiness; the Buddhist dissent.
- [[via-negativa]] — Knowing by negation (neti-neti, la ilaha, divine darkness, the unnameable Tao).
- [[lila]] — Divine play; the "third thing" beyond intentional vs. automatic creation.
- [[remembrance]] — The path is remembering, not acquiring (dhikr, pratyabhijna, anamnesis, teshuvah).
- [[axial-age]] — The worldwide "inward turn," c. 800–200 BCE.
