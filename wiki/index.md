## Entities

### Figures
- [[al-hallaj]] — Sufi martyr (d. 922) executed for "Ana al-Haqq" ("I am the Truth"); emblem of ego-death.
- [[ibn-arabi]] — Andalusian Sufi (1165–1240); associated with Wahdat al-Wujud (Unity of Being).
- [[rumi]] — Sufi poet (1207–1273); posthumous defender of al-Hallaj; whirling-dervish witness.
- [[al-farabi]] — Islamic Neoplatonist (c. 872–950); architect of the Ten Intellects cosmology.
- [[ibn-sina]] — Avicenna (c. 980–1037); emanation as automatic overflow; the soul's exile & return.
- [[plotinus]] — Founder of Neoplatonism (c. 204–270); "The One" and the original emanation cascade.
- [[imam-hussain]] — Martyr of Karbala (680); read esoterically as pure intellect vs. the lower ego.
- [[hermes-trismegistus]] — Legendary Hermetic sage (Thoth↔Hermes); Emerald Tablet, "as above so below."

### Traditions
- [[sufism]] — Islamic mysticism; Ta'wil, Wahdat al-Wujud, Fana, Dhikr.
- [[advaita-vedanta]] — Hindu non-dualism; Atman=Brahman, Maya, the four states.
- [[tayyibi-ismailism]] — Ismaili branch credited with the "Drama in Heaven" cosmology.
- [[hermeticism]] — Western esoteric tradition of Mentalism & Correspondence.

### Texts
- [[upanishads]] — Hindu mystical scriptures (esp. Mandukya); *Gupta Vidya*, the four states.
- [[kybalion]] — 1908 New Thought book codifying the seven Hermetic principles (not actually ancient).

### Symbols
- [[ouroboros]] — Serpent eating its tail; unity of highest and lowest poles.

- [[maya]] — Cosmic illusion; the world of separate objects overlaid on the One.
- [[atman-brahman]] — Self = Absolute; *Aham Brahmasmi*, *Tat Tvam Asi*.
- [[the-observer]] — The witnessing awareness and the "Eternal Now."
- [[seven-hermetic-principles]] — Mentalism, Correspondence, Vibration, Polarity, Rhythm, Cause/Effect, Gender.
