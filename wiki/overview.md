# Overview — Ancient Religions & Esoterica

Top-level orientation for the wiki. The maintainer revises this as sources accumulate and
the synthesis shifts.

## Scope
Ancient religions, mystery traditions, mysticism, and esoteric/occult knowledge — their
deities, figures, texts, places, symbols, rituals, and the doctrines and cosmologies that
connect them. A special interest of this domain is **syncretism**: how the same beings,
symbols, and ideas recur, merge, and transform across traditions.
